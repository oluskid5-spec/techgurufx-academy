Founder & Lead Instructor

Meet the Founder Kunle — Founder, TechGuru Academy Kunle is a technology enthusiast, software engineer and web developer with a passion for helping people develop practical digital skills and create opportunities through technology. As the founder of TechGuru Academy, he is committed to building a learning community where students can acquire practical skills in technology, software development, artificial intelligence, web development and forex trading. His approach to education goes beyond theory. Through hands-on projects, guided learning, mentorship and continuous practice, TechGuru Academy helps students develop skills they can apply to real-world problems, professional careers and entrepreneurship. Kunle believes that with the right knowledge, consistent practice and proper mentorship, anyone can develop valuable skills and build a better future. His mission is simple: Learn. Build. Trade.
